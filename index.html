How CivicFix AI Works

From civic complaint to verified municipal resolution in 4 steps

📷
1. Snap & Report

Capture a photo of the road damage, waste spill, or hazard with automatic GPS location detection.

🤖
2. AI Triage

Gemini AI analyzes the photo to recommend severity, category, priority, and flags nearby duplicates.

🚜
3. Municipal Dispatch

Department officers review the complaint, assign field crews, and dispatch maintenance teams.

✅
4. Track & Resolve

Citizens track the resolution timeline in real-time with full audit logs and photos.
